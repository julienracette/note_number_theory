\chapter{Quadratic Residues}
\intro{
    Which integers are squares modulo an odd prime $p$? Euler's criterion answers the question for a given $a$ by computing a single power, and Gauss's lemma turns it into a counting problem.

    The law of quadratic reciprocity relates the question ``is $p$ a square modulo $q$?'' to the question ``is $q$ a square modulo $p$?''. Together with the Jacobi symbol, it gives a fast way to compute Legendre symbols. The Legendre symbol $\left(\frac{\cdot}{p}\right)$ is also our first example of a non-principal Dirichlet character (see \cref{def:dirchar}).
}
