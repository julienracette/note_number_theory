\chapter{Congruences}
\intro{
    Congruences turn statements about divisibility into computations in the ring $\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$. We describe its units, solve linear congruences, and prove the Chinese remainder theorem.

    The theorems of Fermat, Euler and Wilson close the chapter. The group $(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^\times$ and Euler's function $\phi$ reappear throughout the analytic part, most visibly in the definition of Dirichlet characters (see \cref{def:dirchar}).
}
