\chapter*{Introduction}
This part collects the arithmetic of the integers that the rest of the notes rely on: divisibility and unique factorisation, computations modulo $m$, arithmetic functions and their convolution, the structure of the group $(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^\times$, and quadratic residues. Most results are classical and are proved with elementary tools only; they are used freely in the analytic part.

Throughout, $p$ and $q$ denote prime numbers, $(a,b)$ the greatest common divisor of $a$ and $b$, and $[a,b]$ their least common multiple.
